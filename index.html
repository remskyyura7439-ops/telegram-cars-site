Telegram Cars Board Sync

Telegram Cars Board Sync is a private, single-user automation tool for my own accounts.

What it does

The tool connects to my own Pinterest Business account through the official Pinterest API, reads selected boards and Pins that I have access to, and helps me curate content for publication to my own Telegram channel.

It is not offered to other users, does not scrape Pinterest, and does not sell Pinterest data. When Pinterest content is referenced outside Pinterest, the original Pin/source link is retained for attribution where applicable.

Access

The application is intended only for the developer's own authenticated Pinterest account and personal Telegram channel workflow.
