Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Telegram Cars Board Sync handles data. The application is a private, single-user tool used only by its developer with the developer's own Pinterest Business account and Telegram channel.

Data accessed

Through the official Pinterest API, the application may access account information required for authentication, board identifiers and names, Pin identifiers, titles or descriptions, media references, and source links for Pins that the authenticated account is permitted to access.

How data is used

Data is used only to read and organize the developer's own Pinterest boards and Pins, select content for the developer's own Telegram channel workflow, preserve source attribution, and keep a local history to reduce duplicate publications.

Data sharing and sale

The application does not sell Pinterest data and does not provide Pinterest data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. It is not a multi-user service and does not authenticate external users.

Data retention

Only the minimum data needed for the workflow may be retained, such as Pin IDs, board IDs, source URLs, and publication-history records. Local retained records may be deleted by the developer at any time.

Security

API credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored privately in environment variables or other non-public configuration and are not intentionally published in this website or repository.

Third-party services

The workflow relies on Pinterest and Telegram, which operate under their own terms and privacy policies. This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest or Telegram.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this application can be submitted through the developer's public GitHub profile: github.com/remskyyura7439-ops.
